★Inflation

Food now counts for less in India's price index

CPI inflation, December 2025, and the new CPI series (base 2024=100)

Retail inflation is measured by pricing a fixed basket of what families buy. The old series (base 2012) reflected spending patterns that have changed since 2011-12. So the new series, first used for January 2026 data, carries more items, gives food a much smaller share and gives housing a much bigger one. December 2025 was the last month on the old basket, at just 1.33%.

★Growth

India's first guess for the year: 7.4% growth, up from 6.5%

GDP First Advance Estimates FY2025-26 · NSO (MoSPI) · January 2026

In January 2026, before the financial year was over, the statistics office made its first estimate of how much the economy grew. For FY26 it put real growth at 7.4%, a clear step up from 6.5% the year before. Services led the way: finance, real estate and public administration grew fastest, while farming and utilities lagged far behind.

★Forecasts

Forecasters agree India stays above 6%, but differ on how fast

World Bank Global Economic Prospects · IMF · UN DESA · Economic Survey 2026

India's growth forecast: the ladder

For this year, the IMF is a notch more hopeful than the World Bank. For next year, everyone expects growth to cool to the mid-6s, while the government's own Economic Survey, tabled by FM Nirmala Sitharaman and prepared under CEA V. Anantha Nageswaran, is the most optimistic at 6.8–7.2%.

Remember it like this"IMF one up": IMF 7.3 sits one notch above the World Bank's 7.2 for FY26.
