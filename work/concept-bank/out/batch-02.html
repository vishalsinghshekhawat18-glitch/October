★ Real Estate and Infrastructure Investment Trusts
In plain words: Pooled investment trusts that own income-generating real estate or infrastructure assets and issue tradeable units to investors.

Why it exists

  • Direct acquisition of commercial real estate or infrastructure projects requires enormous capital beyond individual retail reach.
  • Trust structures aggregate distributed savings into professional grade operating assets while providing regular cash distributions.

How it works

  • A corporate sponsor transfers operational commercial property or infrastructure assets into an investment trust.
  • The trust raises capital by issuing units traded freely on stock exchanges to institutional and retail unitholders.
  • Operational earnings from property rentals or infrastructure user tolls are passed through to unitholders as periodic dividends.

Fixed facts

  • DR = foreign-currency instrument issued abroad against Indian securities held with a domestic custodian → lets overseas investors trade Indian units
  • Permitted exchanges: India INX and NSE IX (both IFSC, GIFT City)
  • DRs held only by non-residents
  • Units behind DRs cannot be priced below the price offered to Indian investors for a similar offering
  • Disclosures made on the international exchange → also filed with Indian exchanges within 24 hours; manager appoints a merchant banker
  • Privately placed InvITs: fresh DRs subscribed only by FIIs and corporate bodies

How it changed

  • November 2026: Fresh DR issue → approval of at least 75% of votes cast by unitholders; not needed for a simultaneous initial offer in India + DR listing abroad
  • April 2026: InvITs with net borrowings above 49% of asset value: fresh borrowings allowed for capex to enhance asset performance or capacity, major maintenance of road projects, and refinancing of principal only (regulations amended 17 April 2026)

Traps

  • Depository receipts backed by units can only be held by non-resident investors.
  • The pricing of underlying units cannot fall below the valuation offered to domestic Indian unitholders.

  • Nov 2026: SEBI permits depository receipts (DRs) for REITs and InvITs
  • Apr 2026: SEBI amends InvIT borrowing and SPV rules
