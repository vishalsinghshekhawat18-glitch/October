★ Vibrant Villages Programme

In plain words: A central government initiative develops border villages along northern and international frontiers to upgrade essential connectivity, basic amenities, and prevent border out-migration.

Why it exists

Border communities in isolated frontier valleys face severe isolation and economic hardship, leading to depopulation that compromises borderland security and territorial surveillance.

How it works

The program allocates dedicated capital to selected border settlements, financing all-weather road links, off-grid power, digital internet towers, and community tourism facilities.

Fixed facts

  • Launched 2023; nodal Ministry of Home Affairs; ₹11,639 crore for 2,616+ border villages
  • VVP-I: Centrally Sponsored, ₹4,800 crore, 662 villages in Arunachal, Himachal, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, Ladakh; launched 10 April 2023 at Kibithoo

Traps

The initiative is coordinated directly through the home ministry across two distinct phases covering different states and border frontiers.

In the news

  • VVP
★ Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY and Digital Health Mission

In plain words: A national public health insurance scheme provides cashless secondary and tertiary hospitalization cover to vulnerable families alongside digital health identification records.

Why it exists

Catastrophic health expenses plunge millions of impoverished families into poverty, requiring universal entitlement health cover and portable nationwide digital medical records.

How it works

Eligible beneficiary families receive annual family hospitalization entitlements usable across empaneled public and private hospitals, tracked through unique digital health identification numbers.

Fixed facts

  • AB PM-JAY launched 23 Sep 2018; ₹5 lakh per family; 13.25 crore admissions worth ₹2.03 lakh crore; budget 2026-27 ₹9,500 cr; SECC 2011 based
  • ABDM (Sept 2021): 97.61 crore ABHA IDs, 119.95 crore records (17 Sep 2026)

Traps

Health coverage entitlements apply per family on a floater basis rather than per individual, and records link to unique digital health identifiers.

In the news

  • AB PM-JAY and ABDM at 8 years
★ POSHAN Abhiyaan and Mission Poshan 2.0

In plain words: A holistic national nutrition mission improves maternal and child nutritional outcomes by monitoring early infant growth, dietary diversity, and anganwadi service delivery.

Why it exists

High rates of stunting, wasting, and anemia among young children and mothers impair cognitive growth and human capital development without convergence across welfare departments.

How it works

Frontline anganwadi workers track pregnant women and infants through smartphone applications, coordinating supplementary nutrition and maternal health counseling during early child growth.

Fixed facts

  • Nutrition norms (Schedule II, National Food Security Act 2013) revised Jan 2023; helpline 1515 for POSHAN and PMMVY queries (from Nov 2025)
  • Poshan Tracker launched 1 March 2021; 13.99 lakh Anganwadi Centres, 8.94 crore beneficiaries (31 July 2026); PMMVY completes 10 years

Traps

The mission focuses critically on the initial critical thousand-day developmental window from conception to a child reaching two years of age.

In the news

  • Mission Poshan 2.0 and POSHAN Abhiyaan
★ Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana

In plain words: A nationwide road construction scheme provides all-weather asphalt road connectivity to eligible unconnected rural habitations and upgrades core agricultural transport routes.

Why it exists

Isolated rural habitations cannot access markets, secondary schooling, or emergency healthcare without reliable all-weather paved road transport networks.

How it works

Central allocations finance state rural road development agencies to engineer standard single-lane asphalt roads and river crossings linking rural settlements to highway networks.

Fixed facts

  • Cabinet: continuation beyond March 2025 to March 2028; outlay ₹80,250 cr → ₹83,977 cr; hilly-area bridges till March 2029; 161 long-span bridges (₹961 cr) can be sanctioned
  • Focus: through routes and major rural links to Gramin Agricultural Markets, higher secondary schools, hospitals

Traps

The third phase concentrates on upgrading major through-routes connecting villages to agricultural markets rather than constructing new greenfield village roads.

In the news

  • PMGSY-III extended to March 2028
★ PM Vishwakarma artisan support scheme

In plain words: A central sector initiative provides skill certification, modern toolkits, subsidized collateral-free loans, and digital transaction incentives to traditional artisans and craftspeople.

Why it exists

Traditional artisans work using archaic tools with limited working capital, facing exploitation and loss of traditional craft livelihoods without formal institutional support.

How it works

Enrolled craftspeople receive recognized identity credentials, basic skill upgrades, equipment grants, and tiered low-interest bank credit facilities backed by government credit guarantees.

Fixed facts

  • Identity card and certificate; skill training with stipend ₹500/day; toolkit incentive up to ₹15,000; collateral-free loan up to ₹3 lakh; digital transaction incentives

Traps

Beneficiaries must practice one of eighteen specified traditional artisan trades to qualify for subsidized collateral-free enterprise credit and modern equipment kits.

★ PM SVANidhi street vendors credit scheme

In plain words: A dedicated micro-credit program offers working capital credit facilities to urban street vendors, graduating to larger loan tranches upon timely repayment.

Why it exists

Urban street vendors depend on exploitative informal moneylenders for daily working capital, requiring formal banking credit access and digital financial inclusion.

How it works

Lending institutions disburse small collateral-free working capital loans with interest subsidies, increasing credit limits across successive tranches as vendors repay punctually.

Fixed facts

  • Cabinet 27 Aug 2025: lending extended to 31 Mar 2030; outlay ₹7,332 crore; 1.15 crore beneficiaries (50 lakh new); launched Jun 2020
  • Tranches: ₹10,000 → ₹15,000; ₹20,000 → ₹25,000; third ₹50,000; UPI-linked credit card up to ₹30,000

Traps

Loan limits graduate across successive tiers from initial tranches to higher working capital caps conditional upon confirmed digital repayment records.

In the news

  • PM SVANidhi restructured (till 31 Mar 2030)
★ PM-KISAN income support and Kisan Credit Card

In plain words: Direct income support transfers cash assistance to small landholding farmer households alongside revolving institutional credit facilities for seasonal agricultural inputs.

Why it exists

Smallholder agricultural families need predictable cash transfers to procure farm inputs, avoiding reliance on high-cost unorganized credit for seeds and fertilizer.

How it works

Aadhaar-authenticated direct bank transfers disburse periodic installments directly into farmer bank accounts, complemented by revolving credit lines through bank credit cards.

Fixed facts

  • KCC (1998): 7.72 crore active cards, ≈₹10.2 lakh crore outstanding; MISS crop-loan limit ₹3 lakh → ₹5 lakh; fisheries/allied limit ₹2 lakh → ₹5 lakh; collateral-free ₹1.6 lakh → ₹2 lakh (from 1 Jan 2025); effective rate 4% (7% less 3% prompt-repayment subvention); fisheries KCC extended 2018-19; Kisan Rin Portal Sept 2023; 457 banks onboarded
  • Composite facility of 6 years: short-term crop + allied + post-harvest + consumption + maintenance + insurance + produce marketing, plus long-term investment credit

Traps

Income assistance operates as an outright non-repayable income supplement, whereas credit cards function as revolving bank borrowing lines requiring seasonal settlement.

In the news

  • PM-KISAN 22nd instalment; Kisan Credit Card
★ Jal Jeevan Mission rural tap water supply

In plain words: A nationwide rural utility mission provides functional household tap connections delivering potable drinking water to every rural home through community-managed infrastructure.

Why it exists

Rural women historically walked long distances to collect untreated drinking water, causing health burdens and loss of economic opportunities without piped tap infrastructure.

How it works

Piped village water supply grids pump treated water to individual dwellings, overseen by local village water sanitation committees managing daily operations and water testing.

Fixed facts

  • Cabinet: extended to December 2028; total outlay ₹8.69 lakh crore; central assistance ₹2.08 lakh crore (2019-20) → ₹3.59 lakh crore (+₹1.51 lakh crore)
  • JJM – Har Ghar Jal launched Aug 2019; funds weightage: 30% difficult terrain (DDP, DPAP areas), 10% SC/ST-dominated areas

Traps

The mission focuses on functional household tap connections with adequate pressure and water quality standards, rather than simple public stand-post installation.

In the news

  • Jal Jeevan Mission extended to Dec 2028 (JJM 2.0)
★ Minimum Support Price and market intervention

In plain words: The central government guarantees purchase floor prices for designated agricultural commodities, procuring farm yields through public agencies to protect producers from market crashes.

Why it exists

Volatile post-harvest crop prices cause severe distress sales among farmers, necessitating guaranteed price safety nets and national foodgrain reserve accumulation.

How it works

The economic affairs cabinet committee announces support rates before sowing seasons based on agricultural cost recommendations, buying surplus produce at designated market centers.

Fixed facts

  • PM-AASHA (Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan): launched 2018; MSP assurance for pulses, oilseeds, copra
  • Chhattisgarh: procurement under PM-AASHA with digital farmer registration on E-Samyukti portal; NCCF and NAFED lead

Traps

Support price procurement is implemented primarily for staple grains and pulses, while perishable fruits and vegetables are covered under market intervention mechanisms.

In the news

  • MSP for Rabi crops, Marketing Season 2027-28
  • Structured pulse procurement under Atmanirbhar Pulses Mission
★ Agriculture Infrastructure Fund and farm schemes

In plain words: A dedicated financing facility provides medium-to-long term debt financing with interest subsidies and credit guarantees for post-harvest farm infrastructure and community farm assets.

Why it exists

Post-harvest losses of agricultural produce reduce farmer earnings, requiring modern cold stores, sorting silos, and primary processing centers near farm gates.

Commercial and cooperative banks disburse term loans for eligible post-harvest projects, receiving annual central interest subventions and credit risk guarantees.

Financing under this fund is earmarked specifically for post-harvest management projects and community farming assets rather than standard crop production loans.

★ National Social Assistance Programme pensions

In plain words: A centrally sponsored social welfare programme provides non-contributory monthly pensions to destitute elderly citizens, widows, and persons with severe disabilities living below poverty lines.

Why it exists

Indigent senior citizens and vulnerable persons without family support require regular monthly income transfers to meet basic nutritional and medical survival needs.

How it works

Central allocations transfer social assistance funds to state welfare departments, disbursing monthly pensions directly to eligible beneficiaries through direct benefit transfers.

Fixed facts

  • Union Cabinet: outlay ₹53,428 crore, 2026-27 to 2030-31; 3.20 crore beneficiaries in all 36 States/UTs; nodal: Department of Rural Development.
  • NSAP → Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana. IGNOAPS (old age) → PM Vridhavastha Pension Yojana; IGNWPS (widow) → PM Vidhawa Pension Yojana; IGNDPS (disability) → PM Divyangjan Pension Yojana; National Family Benefit Scheme → PM Parivar Labh Yojana.

Traps

Assistance is targeted strictly to destitute individuals below the poverty threshold, operating as non-contributory welfare rather than funded pension annuities.

In the news

  • NSAP continued for 5 years; schemes renamed
★ AgriStack digital agricultural infrastructure

In plain words: A digital public infrastructure ecosystem generates unique digital identities for farmers linked to geo-referenced land plots, crop registries, and government subsidy databases.

Why it exists

Agricultural credit appraisal, crop insurance verification, and subsidy distribution suffer from paperwork delays and duplicate records without unified digital farm registries.

How it works

States digitize cadastral parcel boundaries and crop surveys, issuing authenticated digital farmer identification numbers that unlock instant access to loans and welfare schemes.

Fixed facts

  • Target of 11 crore Farmer IDs met, 5 months before the March 2027 deadline; phased 6 crore (FY25), 3 crore (FY26), 2 crore (FY27)
  • Mission: Cabinet 2 Sep 2024, outlay ₹2,817 crore (Centre ₹1,940 cr); includes Soil Profile Mapping and Digital General Crop Estimation Survey

Traps

Digital farmer identification facilitates paperless service delivery but does not replace physical title deeds recorded in state revenue departments.

In the news

  • 11 crore Farmer IDs reached (AgriStack)
★ FASTag electronic toll collection system

In plain words: An electronic tolling infrastructure uses radio frequency identification technology attached to vehicle windscreens to deduct highway toll charges automatically from linked bank accounts.

Why it exists

Cash toll collection at highway plazas created severe vehicle congestion, fuel wastage, and revenue leakages without automated non-stop electronic plaza clearing.

How it works

Overhead readers at plaza toll lanes scan RFID tags on passing vehicles, transmitting debit instructions to national toll clearing houses for instant account reconciliation.

Fixed facts

  • Fee ₹3,000 → ₹3,075 from 1 Apr 2026; bought via Rajmargyatra app or NHAI site; activated within 2 hours.
  • Static: FASTag uses RFID; valid 5 years; IHMCL implements, NPCI runs NETC clearing house; all lanes FASTag lanes since 15/16 Feb 2021; no valid tag = 2× fee; helpline 1033.

Traps

Electronic tags link to registered vehicle registration numbers and cannot be transferred between different vehicle chassis.

In the news

  • FASTag Annual Pass crosses 1 crore
★ Dedicated Freight Corridors infrastructure

In plain words: High-capacity electrified railway corridors reserved exclusively for long-distance freight trains connect major industrial hubs and ports with higher speeds and heavy haulage.

Why it exists

Shared tracks between passenger and goods trains cause chronic freight bottlenecks, slow transit times, and excessive industrial logistics costs across national trunk routes.

How it works

Automated signaling, heavy axle-load tracks, and double-stack container clearance allow dedicated freight trains to operate at high sustained speeds between inland ports and terminals.

Fixed facts

  • PM dedicated ₹73,000 cr Western DFC; final 3 sections (326 route km) incl. New Saphale–JNPA; 1,506 km across 5 States (Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Haryana, UP), Dadri → JNPA
  • Eastern DFC 1,337 km (Oct 2023, ₹51,000 cr) → total 2,843 km; project begun 2005 (Manmohan Singh)

Traps

Dedicated freight tracks are engineered with higher axle loads and electrical clearances specifically for freight trains and do not carry scheduled passenger train services.

In the news

  • Western DFC completed
★ National Green Hydrogen Mission

In plain words: A national mission subsidizes domestic electrolyser manufacturing and clean fuel production to establish the country as a global exporter of green hydrogen and its derivatives.

Why it exists

Decarbonizing heavy industrial sectors like steel, refineries, and fertilizer manufacturing requires replacing fossil natural gas with renewable-powered hydrogen.

How it works

Financial incentives disburse production-linked subsidies for green hydrogen plants and domestic electrolyser manufacturing, mandating phased clean fuel blending in domestic industries.

Fixed facts

  • Commitments 5,55,610 MTPA (as of Oct 2026) ≈ 11% of target; 23 offtake contracts since launch (2023), 18 of them (over 4 lakh MTPA) in 2026
  • Over 70% for export; expected investment about ₹90,000 crore

Traps

Production subsidies apply strictly to hydrogen produced through water electrolysis powered by renewable energy or biomass, distinguishing green hydrogen from fossil grey fuel.

In the news

  • National Green Hydrogen Mission: offtake crosses 5.5 lakh tonnes a year
