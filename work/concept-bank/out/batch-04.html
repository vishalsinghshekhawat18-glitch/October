★ World Gold Council demand and reserve tracking

In plain words: The World Gold Council tracks international bullion movements, central bank sovereign reserve accumulations, and jewellery consumption trends across major economies.

Why it exists

Central banks and institutional investors need systematic global surveillance of bullion reserves and physical gold demand to monitor monetary stability and macroeconomic liquidity.

How it works

Quarterly demand surveys synthesize physical buying, bar and coin accumulation, exchange-traded fund activity, and central bank reserve acquisitions across sovereign jurisdictions.

Fixed facts

  • India demand Q3 2026: 162.5 tonnes (down 29% on year); value ₹2.42 lakh crore (up 3%)
  • Bars and coins = India's largest gold investment channel; India 2nd-largest bar and coin market after China, about 21% of global demand
  • Central banks (Q3): China 63 t (largest disclosed buyer); Poland 41 t (2nd); Russia sold 19 t (only major seller)

Traps

Do not confuse value metrics with tonnage, as elevated global gold prices can increase total expenditure even when physical volume demand drops sharply.

In the news

  • World Gold Council: India gold demand Q3 2026; central bank buying

★ Legal Entity Identifier and Unique Transaction Identifier

In plain words: Standardised alphanumeric codes uniquely identify legal entities participating in financial transactions and track individual derivative contracts across global trade repositories.

Why it exists

Financial supervisors need transparent identification of legal counterparties and individual derivative contracts to measure systemic risk exposure and prevent financial fraud.

How it works

Accredited local operating units assign standard codes to financial market participants and derivative deals for centralized reporting to trade repositories.

Fixed facts

  • Master Direction 2026: Section A LEI (Legal Entity Identifier, 20 characters) immediate; Section B UTI (Unique Transaction Identifier) from 1 Jan 2027

How it works

Regulated lenders must publish daily interest rates for large wholesale balances by designated morning timelines, aligning differential returns with liquidity coverage requirements.

Fixed facts

★ Mule account monitoring and digital transaction fraud controls

In plain words: Regulatory standard operating procedures require financial institutions to place temporary debit holds on bank accounts suspected of laundering cyber-fraud proceeds.

Why it exists

Cyber criminals launder illicit proceeds across intermediary mule bank accounts, requiring prompt transaction halts and structured account verification to recover defrauded funds.

How it works

Suspected transactions trigger temporary debit holds for a specified period, giving account holders designated time to provide legitimate evidence before funds are repatriated.

Fixed facts

  • Bank places a Temporary Debit Hold on a suspected money-mule transaction (or entire account) → max 60 days (unless law enforcement says otherwise)
  • Suspected transaction threshold ₹1,000+; holder gets 20 days to explain; bank decides within 10 days of explanation, or 30 days from hold if none

Traps

Remember that temporary debit holds restrict outgoing debits while allowing incoming credits, and unexplained funds are repatriated rather than confiscated by the commercial bank.

★ Nidhi Companies regulatory framework

In plain words: Non-banking mutual benefit financial entities operate exclusively among their members to cultivate thrift, accepting deposits and advancing small loans secured against physical collateral.

Why it exists

Traditional mutual benefit societies enable grassroots financial cooperation among local members, but require strict statutory oversight to protect member savings from unauthorized commercial expansion.

How it works

Regulated under company law, these entities cannot solicit public deposits, transact with non-members, or issue unsecured loans, and must maintain unencumbered reserves with commercial banks.

Fixed facts

  • Nidhi = mutual-benefit company: deposits/loans only from members; Section 406, Companies Act 2013, Nidhi Rules 2014
  • Central Govt declaration (Form NDH-4) mandatory again from 15 Aug 2019; 395 declared Nidhis; deposits not DICGC-insured

Traps

Deposits placed with Nidhi companies are strictly excluded from statutory deposit insurance coverage, distinguishing them from commercial and cooperative bank deposits.

Sovereign monetary authority must maintain public trust in circulating physical currency by ensuring counterfeit-free supply, clean note standards, and systematic withdrawal of soiled paper.

Fluctuating interest rates cause capital losses on fixed-income securities held by banks, historically requiring a dedicated capital buffer to protect solvency.

Fixed facts

The investment fluctuation reserve was specifically designed as a buffer against depreciation in security investments rather than general loan defaults.

In plain words: A principle-based restructuring framework enables commercial lenders to provide loan repayment moratoriums, rescheduled tenors, and interest conversions to borrowers impacted by notified natural disasters.

Natural calamities destroy farmer and business cash flows, requiring immediate institutional debt relief without automatically categorizing stressed borrowers as defaulting non-performing accounts.

Following district or state disaster declarations, lenders restructure standard loans not beyond thirty days overdue, offering repayment extensions and restructuring credit facilities.
