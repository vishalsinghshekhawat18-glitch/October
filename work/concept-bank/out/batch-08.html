★ Semicon India Programme and semiconductor ecosystem

In plain words: A central industrial incentive scheme subsidizes capital expenditure to establish domestic silicon fabrication, packaging, and semiconductor design ecosystems.

Why it exists

Heavy dependence on imported microchips poses critical supply chain vulnerabilities for telecommunications, automotive electronics, and national defence hardware.

How it works

The government provides fiscal support covering capital investments for silicon wafer fabs, assembly and testing facilities, and chip design startups.

Fixed facts

  • Semicon 1.0 (2021): ₹76,000 crore; Semicon 2.0 approved under India Semiconductor Mission: ₹1,27,500 crore
  • Six pillars: 1 chip design; 2 machines and materials; 3 additional fabs; 4 ATMP/OSAT expansion; 5 R&D; 6 talent

Traps

Incentives apply to commercial fabrication units, testing facilities, and approved design startups rather than generic retail computer hardware assembling.

In the news

  • Semicon 2.0 portal launched
  • Semicon India Programme 2.0 and SEMICON India 2026
  • India chip fabrication plant
★ Artemis Accords space cooperation

In plain words: A multilateral non-binding framework guides international civil space exploration and peaceful lunar surface activities in accordance with outer space treaties.

Why it exists

Expanding governmental and private exploration missions to the Moon and Mars require transparent international coordination to prevent territorial conflict.

How it works

Signatory nations commit to peaceful space exploration, transparent mission operations, scientific data sharing, and non-interference around active space hardware.

Fixed facts

  • Artemis Accords: 2020, NASA + US State Department with 7 founding countries; India signed 21 June 2023 as the 27th nation

Traps

The accords represent political principles for peaceful lunar operations rather than creating a binding new military alliance in outer space.

★ Make in India industrial policy

In plain words: A national industrial policy stimulates domestic manufacturing output, attracts direct foreign investment, and expands export capabilities across key economic sectors.

Why it exists

Accelerating economic growth and providing quality employment require expanding manufacturing contribution beyond services within gross domestic value added.

How it works

The framework streamlines bureaucratic licensing, enhances infrastructure logistics, and provides sectoral production-linked fiscal incentives for domestic factory output.

Fixed facts

  • Make in India 2.0: 27 sectors (15 manufacturing + 12 services)
  • Defence production record ₹1.78 lakh crore (FY26); cumulative FDI US$843 bn (2014-15 to 2025-26, +169%)

Traps

The policy covers designated manufacturing and services sectors rather than offering indiscriminate import tariffs across all consumer product lines.

★ Digital India Land Records Modernisation Programme

In plain words: A central sector scheme digitizes cadastral property maps, computerizes registration records, and assigns unique spatial identification codes to land parcels.

Why it exists

Outdated paper land records produce rampant property litigation, title insecurity, and bureaucratic delays in land acquisition for development projects.

How it works

Survey teams map parcel boundaries using drone surveys and satellite imagery, assigning a standardized fourteen-digit numeric code to each registered property.

Fixed facts

  • Ministry of Rural Development; outlay ₹565.50 crore; period 2026–31; 100% central funding
  • 75 Registration Seva Kendras (like Passport Seva Kendras); NAKSHA urban land mapping → UrPro cards; monitored via PS&MC

Traps

Digital parcel identification creates transparent property records without altering underlying state tenancy laws or title dispute jurisdiction.

In the news

  • DILRMP 3.0
★ GOBARdhan scheme and compressed biogas

In plain words: A national welfare initiative converts cattle dung, agricultural crop residues, and organic waste into compressed biogas and bio-slurry fertilizer.

Why it exists

Untreated rural animal waste and stubble burning cause severe environmental pollution while depriving farming households of renewable cooking fuel and income.

How it works

Commercial plants digest rural organic biomass into methane gas, injecting purified fuel into city gas networks while marketing organic fertilizer.

Fixed facts

  • Central Sector scheme, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas; launched by Hardeep Singh Puri; outlay ₹23,731 crore; 10 years (FY2026-27 to FY2035-36)
  • Status (Aug 2026): 217+ plants, 1,700+ TPD capacity

Traps

Subsidies support commercial waste-to-energy processing plants rather than individual household cookstoves without standardized gas compression.

In the news

  • GOBARdhan: ₹23,731 crore Compressed Biogas scheme

★ Coal gasification incentive policy

In plain words: A national industrial incentive scheme finances the thermochemical conversion of domestic coal into synthetic fuel gas, methanol, and chemical feedstocks.

Why it exists

Vast domestic coal reserves can be converted into synthetic petroleum replacements and fertilizers, curbing expensive foreign energy and chemical imports.

How it works

The government provides capital grants to public and private sector plants that gasify thermal coal into syngas under designated project categories.

Fixed facts

  • Scheme: ₹37,500 crore, Cabinet 13 May 2026; target 100 mt gasification by 2030; ~25 projects, ₹2.5–3 lakh crore investment
  • Approved 2024; outlay ₹8,500 crore; cuts import dependence on petroleum, ammonia and fertilisers via syngas

Traps

Incentives require in-situ or surface conversion into chemical syngas and do not apply to conventional coal combustion for thermal power generation.

In the news

  • Coal/lignite gasification scheme; LPG Aadhaar rule
  • Coal gasification incentive scheme

★ Consumer Protection E-Commerce rules

In plain words: Statutory e-commerce consumer regulations mandate transparent online price displays and prohibit manipulative digital pricing practices on digital retail platforms.

Why it exists

Misleading online sales promotions and artificial flash discount countdowns deceive consumers regarding genuine product pricing and prior market rates.

How it works

Regulatory rules require digital sellers to show the lowest prior reference price alongside discounted figures, backed by statutory consumer dispute redressal helplines.

Fixed facts

  • Amends E-Commerce Rules 2020; effective 1 Jan 2027 (Dept of Consumer Affairs)
  • Price-reduction ads must show reduced price + prior price = lowest price in preceding 30 days
  • National Consumer Helpline: toll-free 1915, 17 languages, INGRAM portal

Traps

Prior price disclosures apply to advertised discount reductions rather than prohibiting genuine seller market price fluctuations over time.

In the news

  • Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Amendment Rules, 2026
★ Civil nuclear energy legislation and fast breeder reactors

In plain words: Civil nuclear legislation establishes statutory licensing and civil liability parameters to enable commercial participation in civil nuclear reactor projects.

Why it exists

Scaling carbon-free base load nuclear power requires enabling private capital investment in small modular reactors while securing clear accident liability regimes.

How it works

The statute authorizes central licensing for nuclear facilities, establishes strict graded operator liability limits, and channels sovereign financial backstops above caps.

Fixed facts

  • Sustainable Harnessing and Advancement of Nuclear Energy for Transforming India; Presidential assent 21 Dec 2025
  • Graded operator liability ₹100 cr to ₹3,000 cr by facility type; strict, no-fault liability; Government liability above that up to 300 million SDR
  • PFBR, Kalpakkam (500 MWe; operator BHAVINI) attained criticality; India becomes second country after Russia with a commercial fast breeder reactor once operational

Traps

Operator liability remains strict and no-fault up to statutory caps, backed by sovereign government guarantees rather than unconstrained commercial operator risk.

★ Jan Vishwas decriminalisation of business offences

In plain words: A statutory reform decriminalizes minor procedural lapses and technical business defaults across multiple national statutes, substituting criminal jail with civil fines.

Why it exists

Treating routine clerical errors and regulatory delays as criminal offenses created widespread business harassment and choked judicial dispute dockets.

How it works

Parliamentary amendment schedules remove criminal penal sanctions across commercial statutes, replacing imprisonment with proportionate civil penalties and formal notices.

Fixed facts

  • Amends 79 Acts of 23 ministries; 784 provisions: 717 decriminalised + 67 ease of living; civil penalties replace jail for minor lapses
  • Earlier: Jan Vishwas Act 2023 (42 Acts, 183 provisions); Bill 2025 (18 Aug 2025, 16 Acts, 355 provisions)

Traps

Decriminalisation eliminates penal imprisonment for administrative defaults without exempting deliberate fraudulent actions or serious economic crimes.

In the news

  • Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Bill, 2026
★ Online Gaming regulatory framework

In plain words: Statutory regulations establish registration mandates, age verification safeguards, and grievance redressal mechanisms for online interactive gaming platforms.

Why it exists

Unregulated real-money online gaming apps exposed vulnerable consumers to financial fraud, predatory wagering algorithms, and addictive gaming habits.

How it works

Gaming operators must secure formal government registration certificates, implement transparent player dispute mechanisms, and route regulatory appeals to designated ministries.

Registration rules require statutory game classification and customer dispute resolution rather than instituting blanket prohibitions on legal interactive entertainment.

★ Classical Languages of India status and criteria

In plain words: An official government designation recognizes ancient Indian languages possessing rich independent literary traditions and centuries of documented antiquity.

Why it exists

Preserving ancient indigenous literary heritage and manuscript scholarship requires specialized national institutional funding, academic chairs, and digitisation centres.

How it works

A linguistic committee evaluates candidate languages against antiquity and literary criteria, conferring official classical status to fund dedicated centres of excellence.

Fixed facts

  • Approved by Dept of Higher Education, Ministry of Education. Earlier CoEs (Kannada, Telugu, Malayalam, Odia) are under CIIL (Central Institute of Indian Languages), Mysuru.

Classical designation requires rigorous linguistic antiquity spanning over a millennium rather than representing common modern regional demographic usage.

★ Four Labour Codes consolidation

In plain words: A comprehensive legislative consolidation modernizes and amalgamates numerous central labor laws into four unified national codes covering wages, relations, social security, and workplace safety.

Why it exists

Dozens of fragmented and overlapping colonial-era labor laws created compliance friction, discouraged formal employment, and excluded informal workers from social safety nets.

How it works

Four statutory codes standardize definitions across industries, universalize minimum wage rights, provide portable social security entitlements, and regulate fixed-term hiring.

Fixed facts

  • Who: Ministry of Labour and Employment (Mansukh Mandaviya); 90 days from 28 Sep 2026; industrial clusters in 316 districts, all 36 States/UTs.

Traps

The four codes consolidate existing statutory labor protections into a unified national framework rather than abolishing regulatory workplace safety standards.

★ Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita criminal law codes

In plain words: Three modernized criminal law statutes replace colonial-era penal, criminal procedure, and evidence codes to modernize criminal jurisprudence and expedite justice delivery.

Why it exists

Century-old colonial criminal codes prioritized colonial authority over citizen rights and lacked provisions for electronic evidence, victim rights, and modern investigative methods.

How it works

The reformed statutes modernize offense definitions, establish mandatory timelines for criminal investigations, codify community service sentences, and recognize electronic records.

Fixed facts

  • Three laws in force from 1 Jul 2024: BNS 2023 replaced the IPC (358 sections vs 511); BNSS 2023 replaced the CrPC (531 vs 484); BSA 2023 replaced the Indian Evidence Act (170 provisions vs 167).

Traps

The new statutes apply to criminal offenses committed on or after the official enactment date rather than applying retroactively to pending historical trials.
