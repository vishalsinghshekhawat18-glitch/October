★ G4 diplomatic coalition for UN Security Council reform

In plain words: An international diplomatic group of four nations mutually supports each other bids for permanent seats on the United Nations Security Council.

Why it exists

The current post-war composition of the security council excludes major democratic powers and emerging economic regions from permanent decision-making.

How it works

The member countries coordinate diplomatic lobbying, sponsor joint reform resolutions in the general assembly, and advocate expanding both permanent and non-permanent seats.

Fixed facts

  • G4 = India, Brazil, Germany, Japan (formed 2005), meeting at UNGA 81
  • UNSC: 15 members; P5 (US, UK, France, Russia, China) with veto; 10 non-permanent elected for 2 years

Traps

The coalition focuses on permanent representation reform rather than general macroeconomic trade policy.

★ Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam civil aviation regulations

In plain words: A unified civil aviation regulatory framework establishes modern licensing, aircraft operations, and tariff oversight rules replacing pre-independence aircraft provisions.

Why it exists

Decades-old colonial aviation rules created regulatory fragmentation and administrative delays for rapid modern civil aerospace expansion.

How it works

The ministry of civil aviation notifies updated operational standards, while the statutory aviation regulator oversees carrier compliance and passenger tariffs.

Fixed facts

  • Replaces Aircraft Rules, 1937 (nearly nine decades old)
  • Notified by Ministry of Civil Aviation; in force 1 Jan 2027

Traps

The new regulations modernize operating standards and oversight rather than abolishing civil aviation safety checks.

In the news

  • Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam, 2026 replaces Aircraft Rules, 1937
★ Dearness Allowance and Dearness Relief mechanism

In plain words: A statutory inflation-adjustment compensatory allowance protects government employees and pensioners from erosion of real purchasing power caused by inflation.

Why it exists

Continuous consumer price inflation reduces the real value of fixed government salaries and retirement pensions without periodic cost-of-living adjustments.

How it works

The union cabinet announces periodic percentage rate increases based on industrial worker consumer price index trends, disbursing arrears twice yearly.

Fixed facts

  • DA (employees) and DR (pensioners): 60% → 63% of basic pay/pension; effective 1 Jul 2026
  • Formula per 7th Central Pay Commission; revised twice a year (January, July)

Traps

Dearness allowance applies to serving government staff while dearness relief applies to retired pensioners, both revised on the same formula.

In the news

  • Cabinet: DA and DR raised 3% to 63% from 1 July 2026
  • DA up 3 points to 63%; four Cabinet approvals

★ Urban Challenge Fund and credit guarantees

In plain words: A central incentive mechanism provides catalytic funding and credit guarantees to municipal bodies undertaking bankable urban infrastructure and civic projects.

Why it exists

Municipal governments face persistent capital shortages and weak municipal credit ratings, preventing private bond financing for urban development.

How it works

The union government co-finances viable urban infrastructure projects, providing repayment credit guarantees for smaller and hilly city municipal debt.

The fund requires municipal bodies to mobilize private capital and bond finance rather than relying entirely on non-repayable central grants.

★ PM RAHAT cashless road accident victim treatment

In plain words: A national road safety scheme ensures immediate cashless medical stabilization and trauma care for road traffic accident victims at designated hospitals.

Why it exists

Delay in critical trauma care during the golden hour after highway accidents leads to preventable fatalities due to hospital admission disputes.

How it works

Emergency dispatchers route victims to empaneled medical centers, providing immediate cashless inpatient care reimbursable through central statutory transport funds.

Fixed facts

  • Launched 13 Feb 2026: cashless treatment up to ₹1.5 lakh per victim for 7 days from accident, on any category of road; Ministry of Road Transport and Highways
  • Stabilisation up to 24 h (48 h if life-threatening); links eDAR (MoRTH) with TMS 2.0 (National Health Authority) and 112 ERSS; paid from Motor Vehicle Accident Fund (insured: general insurers' contribution; uninsured/hit-and-run: Government budget); claims paid within 10 days

Traps

Cashless emergency treatment is provided during the critical stabilization window rather than covering indefinite long-term medical care.

  • How PM RAHAT works (arrow chain)

☆ Project Dolphin and aquatic species conservation

Why it exists

Industrial pollution, river damming, and fishing net entanglement threaten freshwater and coastal marine cetacean populations.

How it works

Forest departments patrol riverine sanctuaries, deploy acoustic tracking devices, and engage fishing communities to release accidentally trapped animals.

Fixed facts

  • Ganges River Dolphin ("Susu"): National Aquatic Animal 2009; IUCN Endangered; CITES Appendix I; Wildlife Protection Act 1972 Schedule I

Traps

The Ganges river dolphin is virtually blind and navigates via echolocation rather than visual hunting in murky river waters.

  • Project Dolphin: second range-wide dolphin survey

Poaching pressures and habitat fragmentation endanger wild big cat populations without standardized sanctuary management standards.
