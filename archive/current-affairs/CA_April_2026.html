Insurance

★ IRDAI: Ind AS for insurers from 1 April 2026
In plain words: The insurance regulator is changing how insurers prepare their accounts to a standard used in many countries. This makes their reports easier to compare, and insurers get time to switch over.
  • IRDAI (Actuarial, Finance and Investment Functions of Insurers) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, approved at its 135th meeting
  • Applies to all insurers: Life, General, Stand-Alone Health, reinsurers; earlier IGAAP
  • Parallel reporting (Ind AS plus existing framework) for 2 years; one-year forbearance if an insurer cannot comply
  • Joint expert group on Ind AS transition, chaired by IRDAI's Member (Finance and Investment), for two years
  • Ind AS: converged with IFRS; implemented by Ministry of Corporate Affairs
★ Domestic Systemically Important Insurers (D-SIIs) FY2025-26
In plain words: Some insurers are so large that their failure would shake the whole financial system, so they are watched more closely. The same three insurers kept this label this year.
  • LIC, GIC Re, New India Assurance retain status (too big to fail by size, importance, interconnectedness)
Remember: GIC Re is the state-owned reinsurer; LIC and New India are the largest life and non-life insurers.
