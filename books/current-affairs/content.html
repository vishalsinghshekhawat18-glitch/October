In plain words

India and the European Union finished a free trade deal after nearly 20 years of talks. It lowers barriers on goods and services and opens parts of insurance and banking.

  • Talks launched 2007, relaunched 2022, concluded 26 Jan 2026; EU = India's 22nd FTA partner
  • EU banks allowed 15 branches over 4 years (earlier offer 12)
  • India binds FDI in insurance at 100%; EU eases visas for Indian professionals; AYUSH practitioners allowed
  • Trade: India–EU goods trade USD 136.53 bn (2024-25; exports 75.85, imports 60.68); EU is the largest goods partner; services trade ~USD 83 bn (2024)
  • EU FDI in India 2024: over €132 bn (leading investor)
  • Context: EU leaders Costa and von der Leyen were chief guests at the 77th Republic Day (26 Jan); 16th India–EU Summit on 27 Jan 2026 (15th was virtual, 15 Jul 2020); strategic partners since 2004
  • Mar 2026: India and EU grant each other MFN status for 5 years from FTA entry into force (review in 4th year; auto-terminates unless extended)
  • India–Euratom cooperation on peaceful nuclear energy agreed. Euratom = European Atomic Energy Community, set up by Treaty of Rome 1957
  • India's access: 97% of tariff lines (99.5% of trade value); India offers 92.1% of lines (97.5% of EU exports); social security agreements framework in 5 years; EU 144 services subsectors; India = 4th, EU = 2nd largest economies (25% of world GDP)
